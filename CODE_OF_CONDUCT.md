# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in Zerlyth are expected to be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent while focusing discussion on the code, the evidence, and the user impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Reports should be directed privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
